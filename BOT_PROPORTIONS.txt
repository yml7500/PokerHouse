CALLING & FOLD TOLERANCE PARAMETERS
================================================================================
Location: src/engine/ai.ts

A. BASE_FOLD_MARGIN
   - Values:
       caller:    0.14
       aggressor: 0.08
       shark:     0.04
       rock:      0.00
   - Definition:
       How much worse than pot-odds-required equity a player is willing to
       continue/call with on the flop. Measures "bad-call tolerance" or
       willingness to float speculative holdings.
   - Formula:
       threshold = requiredEquity - foldMargin
       willCall  = (equity + jitter() + 0.001) >= threshold

       A positive margin lowers the bar needed to call. For example, facing a
       half-pot bet (requiredEquity = 25%):
         * Caller requires only >= 11% equity
         * Aggressor requires >= 17% equity
         * Shark requires >= 21% equity
         * Rock strictly requires >= 25% equity

B. streetFactor (Dynamic Decay Across Streets)
   - Formula:
       streetFactor = flop: 1.0 | turn: 0.6 | river: 0.2
       foldMargin   = BASE_FOLD_MARGIN[personalityId] * streetFactor
   - Function:
       Reflects poker mathematics: implied odds exist on the flop, shrink on the
       turn (1 card left to come), and vanish on the river (no more cards left).
       On the river, all bots must have close to true mathematical pot odds.
   - Effective Margins Across Streets:
       Flop:  Caller +14.0% | Aggressor +8.0% | Shark +4.0% | Rock 0.0%
       Turn:  Caller  +8.4% | Aggressor +4.8% | Shark +2.4% | Rock 0.0%
       River: Caller  +2.8% | Aggressor +1.6% | Shark +0.8% | Rock 0.0%

3. BETTING, RAISING & BLUFFING PARAMETERS
================================================================================
Location: src/engine/ai.ts

A. minEdge
   - Values: rock: 0.18 | caller: 0.13 | aggressor: 0.09 | shark: 0.10
   - Definition:
       The minimum equity surplus over pot odds (equitySurplus = equity - requiredEquity)
       required before a bot is eligible to bet or raise for value.
   - Function:
       High values (Rock: 18%) require large statistical edges. Low values
       (Aggressor: 9%) allow attacking with thin advantages.

B. chance
   - Values: rock: 0.25 | caller: 0.24 | aggressor: 0.40 | shark: 0.32
   - Definition:
       The probability that an eligible bot will pull the trigger on a value
       bet or raise rather than checking or flat-calling (pot control/trapping).

C. CHECK_RAISE_CHANCE_MULTIPLIER
   - Value: 0.23 (23%)
   - Definition:
       A multiplier applied to `chance` when facing a check-raise opportunity:
       (player.lastAction === 'check' && callAmt > 0).
   - Function:
       Check-raising is a situational trap in real poker; dampening by 0.23
       prevents bots from check-raising uncontrollably.

D. potFraction
   - Values: rock: 0.33 | caller: 0.33 | aggressor: 0.50 | shark: 0.45
   - Definition:
       The target bet/raise sizing as a proportion of total pot after calling:
         potAfterCall = pot + callAmt
         raiseAmountOnTop = max(minRaise, round(potAfterCall * potFraction))

E. bluffChance
   - Values: rock: 0.03 | caller: 0.03 | aggressor: 0.09 | shark: 0.08
   - Definition:
       The probability of firing a bluff bet when checked to (canCheck === true)
       with weak holdings (equity < 0.40).
   - Safeguards:
       1. Bluff size is capped at <= 40% pot: min(raiseProfile.potFraction, 0.4)
       2. Bluffs are strictly forbidden from pushing all-in.

4. PREFLOP HAND SELECTION (BILL CHEN FORMULA)
================================================================================
Location: src/engine/handRating.ts

A. MIN_CHEN_SCORES
   - Values: rock: 5.5 | shark: 4.5 | aggressor: 3.5 | caller: 3.0
   - Definition:
       Minimum Bill Chen score (0 to 20) required to enter a pot preflop facing
       a bet or blind.

B. Bill Chen Scoring Rules:
   1. High Card Score:
        Ace = 10, King = 8, Queen = 7, Jack = 6, 10 = 5
        9 through 2 = rank / 2 (e.g. 9 -> 4.5, 7 -> 3.5, 2 -> 1.0)
   2. Pocket Pairs:
        Double the high card score, minimum pair score floor is 5.
        (e.g. 22 = 5, 88 = 8, TT = 10, AA = 20)
   3. Suited Cards:
        Add +2.0 points if cards share the same suit.
   4. Gaps / Closeness:
        0 gap (connected, e.g. J-T) = 0
        1-card gap (e.g. J-9)        = -1.0
        2-card gap (e.g. J-8)        = -2.0
        3-card gap (e.g. J-7)        = -4.0
        4+ card gap                  = -5.0
   5. Straight Bonus:
        +1.0 point if gap <= 1 and highest card is less than Queen.

C. Preflop Raise Sizing Penalty:
   When facing a raise above 1 Big Blind (callAmt > bigBlind):
     raisesAboveBB = max(0, (callAmt - bigBlind) / bigBlind)
     requiredChen  = baseThreshold + (raisesAboveBB * 1.0)

5. BANKROLL & BET SIZING MECHANICS (KELLY CRITERION)
================================================================================
Location: src/engine/kelly.ts & src/engine/ai.ts

A. Raw Kelly Equation:
     f* = p - (q / b) = (b * p - q) / b
     where:
       p = win probability / equity in [0, 1]
       q = 1 - p (loss probability)
       b = net odds = potBeforeCall / callAmount

B. MAX_BANKROLL_FRACTION
   - Value: 0.08 (8% of bankroll)
   - Function:
       Absolute hard cap on stack commitment on any single decision,
       preventing stack burnout within a handful of hands.

C. kellyMultiplier
   - Values: aggressor: 0.50 | caller: 0.35 | shark: 0.30 | rock: 0.15
   - Function:
       Scales the raw Kelly fraction down to conservative fractional Kelly:
         recommendedFraction = min(max(0, f*) * kellyMultiplier, 0.08)
         kellyHardCap        = max(minRaise, round(recommendedFraction * bankroll))
         raiseAmountOnTop    = min(raiseAmountOnTop, kellyHardCap)

6. DECISION JITTER & RANDOM NOISE
================================================================================
Location: src/engine/ai.ts

A. jitter(spread = 0.05)
   - Formula: (Math.random() * 2 - 1) * spread
   - Definition: Continuous uniform random noise in [-0.05, +0.05].
   - Function:
       Injected into equity comparisons (equity + jitter()) so bots do not
       act as rigid, perfectly predictable computers.

7. HAND STRENGTH & BOARD PAIR DISQUALIFICATION GATES
================================================================================
Location: src/engine/ai.ts

A. Board Pair Disqualification (heroContributed):
   - Logic:
       isPocketPair = holeCards[0].rank === holeCards[1].rank
       pairsBoard   = holeCards.some(c => boardRanks.has(c.rank))
       heroContributed = isPocketPair || pairsBoard || (solved.rank >= 3)
   - Function:
       Prevents bots from assuming they hold a made hand when the board itself
       has a pair (e.g. board 8-8-K with 3-4 in hand).

B. River Dead-Draw Suppression:
   - Logic: hasDraw = (street !== 'river') && (draws.flush || draws.straight)
   - Function:
       Draw bonuses are deactivated on the river because all 5 cards are dealt.

C. Action Thresholds:
   - equity > 0.78: Triggers value raise eligibility regardless of pot odds.
   - equity > 0.82: Monster hand threshold; permits all-in shoves.
   - stack <= bigBlind * 4: Critically short stack; allows all-in shove.
   - stack * 0.45: Stack cap on non-monster raises to allow multi-street play.
   - equity < 0.30 and !hasGenuineStrength: Postflop air fold gate.

8. ANALYTICAL EQUITY MODEL (fastEstimateEquity)
================================================================================
Location: src/engine/simulator.ts (Used by high-throughput calibration engine)

A. Preflop Formula:
     headsUpEquity = 0.32 + max(0, chen) * 0.0265
     multiway      = headsUpEquity ^ (1 + (numOpponents - 1) * 0.35)
     clamped to: [0.06, 0.92]

B. Postflop Made Hand Base Strengths:
     High Card:         0.18
     One Pair:
       Flop (3 cards):  0.46
       Turn (4 cards):  0.36
       River (5 cards): 0.28 (degrades naturally across streets)
       Unassisted Board:0.18 (treated as High Card)
     Two Pair:          Flop/Turn 0.70 | River 0.60
     Three of a Kind:   0.82
     Straight:          0.90
     Flush:             0.93
     Full House:        0.97
     Quads / St. Flush: 0.99

C. Draw Bonus (Rule of 2 & 4) (when communityCards.length < 5):
     multiplier = flop ? 0.04 : 0.02
     Flush Draw (9 outs):    +9 * multiplier (+36% on flop, +18% on turn)
     Straight Draw (8 outs): +8 * multiplier (+32% on flop, +16% on turn)

D. Multiway Discounting:
     equity = baseStrength / (1 + (numOpponents - 1) * 0.42)
     clamped to: [0.04, 0.98]
